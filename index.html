Supporting Inclusivity, Dignity, and Integrity for Persons with Disabilities

Empowering Communities, Promoting Equality, Creating Change.

SIDAI is a non-political, independent and non-profit Community Based Organization based in Maralal, Samburu County. SIDAI is a Samburu word which means Goodness and also an abriviation which stands for Supporting, Inclusivity, Dignity, and Integrity. We mobilize communities to promote gender equality, social change and inclusion while supporting vulnerable groups.

About Us

Community-led action for lasting social transformation

SIDAI works with communities to promote a conducive environment that fosters equality, support, inclusion, dignity, and integrity for persons with disabilities, while advancing meaningful and sustainable social change.

Our Purpose

SIDAI mobilizes community members towards promoting gender equality and social change.

The organization seeks to build a supportive community for young girls, persons living with disabilities, children, youth, widows and other vulnerable groups.

  • Promote gender equality
  • Support vulnerable groups
  • Promote social inclusion
  • Empower women and girls
  • Strengthen community participation

What We Do

Our Programs & Activities

SIDAI implements community-focused activities designed to empower people, strengthen resilience and promote positive social change.

Community Empowerment

Conduct quarterly community forums to discuss gender rights, social inclusion and harmful practices.

Target: 500 participants per quarter

Sisterhood Circle

Monthly support group meetings for girls and teen mothers, promoting shared learning, emotional support and mentorship.

Target: 30–50 regular attendees

Skills Development

Provide vocational training including beadwork, tailoring and digital literacy to women and girls.

Target: 70 women & girls annually

Youth Empowerment

Facilitate leadership and advocacy workshops and support youth-led community awareness campaigns.

Target: 1,000 youth annually

Peer-to-Peer Counselling

Train peer counsellors in basic psychosocial support and GBV awareness.

Target: 30 peer counsellors/year

Widow Empowerment

Support economic empowerment through small grants, savings groups and entrepreneurship mentorship.

Target: 100 widows annually
